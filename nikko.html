Paced for someone who has trouble walking. Each stop's Directions button opens walking directions from wherever you are.

1 · ~10:30–11:15

Kanmangafuchi Abyss 憾満ヶ淵

Free · open all day · ~30–45 min
Directions
  • Riverside path lined with dozens of moss-covered jizo statues in red caps ("Bake Jizo" — legend says the count changes every time you count them).
  • Path is uneven stone and gravel — go slowly; you can turn back anywhere.
  • Few facilities — no restroom on the path. Stay near other visitors (bears live in the area).
2 · ~11:45

Shinkyo Bridge 神橋

Free to view from the road · 2 min from the ryokan
Directions
  • The red lacquered bridge over the Daiya River — best photo from the road bridge next to it.
  • Walking onto the bridge itself costs a small fee; not needed for the photo.
3 · ~12:00–12:45

Lunch: yuba 湯波

Nikko's specialty — fresh tofu skin
Find nearby
  • Plenty of yuba and soba places around Shinkyo and along the main street. Eat before heading up — food is scarce inside the shrine area.
  • Use the restroom here too.
4 · ~1:00–1:40

Rinno-ji Sanbutsudo + Shoyoen 三仏堂・逍遥園

¥900 Sanbutsudo + Taiyuin combo · ¥300 garden + treasure hall
Directions
  • Buy the combo ticket here — it also covers Taiyuin (stop 7).
  • Hall with three giant gilded Buddhas. Shoyoen garden is small and flat, and the maples should be at their best this week.
  • From the ryokan the approach is uphill; take a taxi if energy is low.
5 · ~1:45–2:45 · enter by 3:30

Toshogu Shrine 日光東照宮

¥1,600 · 9:00–4:00 · ~1 hour
Directions
  • Must-sees, all on the lower level: five-story pagoda, three wise monkeys (on the stable), Yomeimon Gate, and the sleeping cat carved above the gate to the inner shrine.
  • Skip the climb behind the sleeping cat — about 200 stone steps up to Ieyasu's tomb.
  • The main shrine requires removing shoes; there are benches.
6 · ~2:50–3:10

Futarasan Shrine 二荒山神社

Grounds free · ¥300 inner garden · 5 min west of Toshogu
Directions
  • Follow the cedar-lined path west from Toshogu — fairly level.
  • Shrine to good relationships; quick visit is enough.
7 · ~3:15 · enter by 3:30

Taiyuin 大猷院

Covered by the combo ticket · next to Futarasan
Directions
  • Quieter, darker mausoleum of the 3rd shogun — many staircases between its gates.
  • First stop to cut if you're running late or tired; whoever's tired can wait at the entrance.
8 · ~3:45–4:00

Back down the west approach 西参道

Downhill to the ryokan, ~15 min
Directions
  • Be back before dark (~4:30). Main-street shops close around 5 PM.
