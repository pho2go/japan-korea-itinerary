🌰 Obuse afternoon

Tue Nov 17 · Hokusai, chestnuts and old-town lanes · about 2.5 hours, back to Nagano before the after-school crowd

Start 1:35 PM
Train back 3:55 PM
Arrive Nagano ~4:20 PM
Walking route in Google Maps

The town center is flat and compact: the museums, Chestnut Alley and the sweet shops are all within a few minutes of each other. The station is a 12-minute walk from the Hokusai Museum; a shop or the museum desk can call a taxi for anyone who would rather ride. Each Directions button opens directions from wherever you are.

1 · 1:35–2:25 PM

Hokusai Museum 北斎館

9 AM–5 PM, last entry 4:30 · ¥800
Directions
  • Hokusai came to Obuse four times in his 80s as the guest of the merchant Takai Kozan. The highlight is the two festival floats with his ceiling paintings.
  • Buy single tickets — this plan skips the Obuse Museum, so the combo doesn't pay off.
  • Allow about 50 minutes; there are benches inside.
2 · 2:25–2:45 PM

Chestnut Alley and the open gardens 栗の小径

Starts right outside the museum · flat, about 100 m
Directions
  • Best photo spot in town: the lane is paved with chestnut-wood blocks, lined with chestnut trees and old white-walled storehouses.
  • Look for "Welcome to My Garden" signs — residents open their private gardens to visitors. Go in quietly and keep to the paths.
  • The side alleys off the main street are worth a short wander for more shots.
3 · 2:45–3:10 PM

Takai Kozan Memorial Museum 高井鴻山記念館

At the far end of Chestnut Alley · 9 AM–5 PM, last entry 4:30 · cash only
Directions
  • The home of Hokusai's patron. Small — 20–30 minutes is enough.
  • Yuzenro, the two-story wooden salon where Kozan hosted artists, is open — the upstairs room is the part to see (steep wooden stairs; fine to skip).
4 · 3:10–3:30 PM

Chestnut sweets and souvenirs 栗菓子

Main street by the museums
Directions
  • Obusedo (小布施堂) — chestnut Mont Blanc and chestnut sweets; the autumn "Suzaku" is famous but sells out early.
  • Sakurai Kanseido (桜井甘精堂) — chestnut yokan, an easy gift to carry home.
  • Closed today: Senseki, the garden restaurant, is closed on Tuesdays.
5 · 3:30–3:45 PM · train at 3:55

Walk to Obuse Station, buy tickets 小布施駅

About 12 min on foot · taxi ~3 min
Directions
  1. At the machine, press 長野 (Nagano) ¥680 and the number of people. Pay cash.
  2. Add a limited express ticket (特急券) for each person — machine or the staffed window. Show staff: 長野まで 特急で ○枚 (to Nagano, limited express, ○ tickets).
  3. Check the board for 特急 長野 15:55. The ride is about 25 minutes, arriving around 4:20 PM — just before the after-school crowd.
  4. At Nagano, the Nagaden station is underground next to JR. Put the tickets in the exit gate (they are kept) and follow signs up to the JR station and the hotel.

The 7:08 PM and 10:41 PM trains stop at Suzaka and don't reach Nagano — check that the board says 長野.

Busiest stretch is roughly 4–6:30 PM with students and commuters. Limited express trains stay comfortable.

If you take the 5:10 instead

  • Obuse Museum / Nakajima Chinami Gallery (おぶせミュージアム) — buy the ¥1,300 combo at the first museum if you'll do all three.
  • Chikuma River embankment (千曲川), a short walk west of the station — open views of the Northern Nagano mountains, including Mt. Iizuna. Sunset is about 4:35 PM, so go around 4:00.
  • Ganshoin (Hokusai's phoenix ceiling) and Jokoji (600-year-old temple, mossy steps) are on the edge of town — taxi only, and not both.
