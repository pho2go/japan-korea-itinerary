Open decisions

  • Nov 20 tour route. The guide offers (1) Shirakawa-go at a relaxed pace with a soba lunch he can reserve, (2) Shirakawa-go + one Gokayama village, or (3) Okuhida Onsen-go, Hirayu and Ankokuji Temple. A newer option: Shirakawa-go or Shinhotaka Ropeway in the morning, then Okuhida, Hida Folk Village or Takayama Jinya after lunch. Some of the group have already seen Shirakawa-go.
  • Nov 15 vs. Nov 17. One plan puts Togakushi on Nov 15 and Narai-juku on Nov 17; the paid bus is for Nov 16. Agree on the final order.
  • Nov 23 flight. One group plan says Delta to Korea; this itinerary's booking is Air Busan BX718. Confirm everyone's flight.

Possible duplicate bookings

  • Nov 12: a GetYourGuide Nikko tour ($433.50, 8 AM, charged Nov 9) is still booked alongside the Sun Taxi van. It allows no luggage and returns to Tokyo. Cancel before the Nov 9 auto-charge.
  • Nov 18: 3 seats on bus 26513 (10:55) in addition to the group's 6 seats on bus 26507 (13:05).

Money to plan for

  • Nov 12 van: ¥12,500 per person in cash, ¥13,500 with the likely overtime hour (card adds 5%).
  • Japanese hotels may add a city tax per person per night.
  • Entrance fees and meals aren't included on the tours; bring cash.

Good to know

  • Kamikochi closes for winter from Nov 16.
  • Public buses Takayama ↔ Shirakawa-go are sold out Nov 19–21.
  • Luggage forwarding (Yamato "takkyubin") is available at hotel front desks; book the day before, delivery is usually next day.
  • Matsumoto Station has coin lockers for the castle visit on Nov 18.
  • All three booked tours use English-speaking guides.
  • Korea entry: K-ETA is waived for US passports through Dec 31, 2026, but the e-Arrival Card is mandatory and opens only 72 hours before arrival (Nov 20–23).
  • Nov 23 is a Japanese public holiday: Skyliner runs a holiday timetable, so keep the reserved seat.
