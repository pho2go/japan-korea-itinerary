More detail: tax-free step, Suica, arriving in Busan, phrases

Tax-free refund (new since Nov 1)

  • Shops now charge the full price. The tax comes back after customs confirms at the airport that you are taking the goods out of Japan.
  • Do it before checking bags. The kiosks are on the 4th floor of Terminal 1, before security. Each traveler scans their own passport.
  • You can also do it on your phone with Visit Japan Web while connected to the airport's Customs_WiFi_For_TaxRefund network.
  • If one item from a purchase is missing, that whole purchase loses its refund.
  • The refund goes to whatever you registered at the shop (usually a card). For shops that pay cash, the refund counter is also on 4F.

Suica

  • Returning it is optional. A regular Suica stays valid for 10 years after its last use.
  • A Welcome Suica (tourist version) has no deposit and no refund.

Arriving at Gimhae

  • The information desk is on 1F opposite the arrivals exit.
  • Korea is card-first: pay in won, never USD. Cash is mainly for markets, street food and T-money top-ups.
  • On buses, tap the card when you get on and again when you get off.
  • Evening: Haeundae Beach and the market street are a short walk from the hotel.
