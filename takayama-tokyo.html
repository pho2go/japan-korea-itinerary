1 · 7:00–10:30 AM

Last morning in Takayama 高山桜庵

Breakfast, a last onsen soak, pack · check-out is 11:00 but leave by 10:30
Market
  • Early riser? The Miyagawa morning market by the river is a 10-minute walk — good for a last snack or souvenir. Be back by 9:30. It'll be below freezing first thing; sidewalks may be icy in the shade.
  • Suitcases sent by Yamato yesterday? Keep the receipt slip in your day bag — Crown Hills will ask for the name on the label. If you didn't send them, they come on the train (see step 3).
  • Check out at the front desk. Return the shoe-locker key (下足箱) with the room key and collect your shoes at the entrance.
  • Day bag: tickets, passports, a snack and water, and a jacket you can take off — trains are warm, Tokyo will be milder.
2 · 10:30–10:45 AM

Walk to JR Takayama Station JR高山駅

About 5–10 min, flat · taxi if the sidewalks are icy
Directions
  1. Before leaving the hotel: ask the front desk "Takayama-sen wa futsū ni ugoite imasu ka?" (Is the Takayama Line running normally?) — or check the departure board as soon as you walk in.
  2. If it's icy or the walk is too much with bags, ask the desk to call a taxi: "Takayama eki made takushī o onegai shimasu." It's a very short ride.
  3. At the station, find ひだ3号 富山 11:03 on the departure board — it shows the track number (番線).
  4. Feed the fare ticket and the Hida ticket together into the gate and take both back. If the gate rejects them, show them at the staffed window beside the gates.
  5. Go to the platform by about 10:50. Find the car 10 (10号車) marker. Some Hida trains split or join cars at Takayama, so check the number on the car's door before boarding.
  6. If the line is suspended by snow: don't wait on the platform. Go to the JR ticket office (みどりの窓口) with all your tickets and ask for the best way to Tokyo today — usually south via Nagoya and the Tokaido Shinkansen. Your seats and fare can be changed there.
3 · 11:03 AM → 12:32 PM

Limited Express Hida 3 to Toyama 特急ひだ3号

Car 10 · about 1½ hours · Toyama is the last stop
Route
  1. Board car 10 and sit in your reserved seats. Big suitcases go behind the last row of seats or at the end of the car; carry-ons on the overhead rack.
  2. The train follows the Miyagawa and Jinzu river gorges north through the mountains — the best scenery of the day, probably with fresh snow.
  3. This is the only Hida to Toyama in the morning, which is why it's busy. Toyama is the end of the line, so there's no rush to get off — just don't leave anything on board.
  4. Use the train's restroom before arriving if you need it; Toyama's are also easy to find.
4 · 12:32–1:11 PM · 39-min change

Change at Toyama for the Shinkansen 富山駅

Kagayaki 532 leaves from Track 12 · car 5 · elevators all the way
Map
  1. Getting off: follow the green signs 新幹線 / Shinkansen to the Shinkansen transfer gate (新幹線のりかえ口). Don't go out through a regular exit.
  2. Feed all three tickets together (fare + Hida + Kagayaki). The gate keeps the Hida ticket and returns the fare and Kagayaki tickets — take both. If the gate won't take them, use the staffed window next to it.
  3. Lunch inside the Shinkansen area: look for an ekiben (station bento) shop. Toyama's classic is masu-zushi (pressed trout sushi in a round wooden box). Eat it on the train.
  4. Go up to Track 12 by about 1:00 PM. The Shinkansen platforms have safety doors; find the car 5 (5号車) marker and check the board shows かがやき 532 · 13:11 · 東京.
  5. Plenty of time: the walk is 5–10 minutes. Only if you have 20+ minutes spare and want to browse: the Tōyamarché shops are outside the gates — but you'd need staff help to re-enter, so we suggest staying inside.
5 · 1:11 PM → 3:14 PM

Hokuriku Shinkansen Kagayaki 532 かがやき532号

Car 5 · all seats reserved · stops: Nagano 1:56 → Omiya 2:55 → Ueno 3:14
Route
  1. Board car 5 and find your seats. Big suitcases go behind the last row of seats; there's also a little space at the car ends.
  2. Sit back — it's about 2 hours. You'll pass back through Nagano (1:56) and Omiya (2:55).
  3. Ueno is the stop after Omiya. Once you leave Omiya (2:56), start getting coats on and bags down. The train only stops about one minute at Ueno.
  4. Get off at Ueno, not Tokyo. The train continues to Tokyo Station (3:20) — staying on means backtracking.
6 · 3:14–3:35 PM

Ueno Station → the hotel 上野駅

Arrive Track 22, 4 floors underground · exit by the Main Entrance (正面玄関口) · 3–7 min walk
Directions
  1. Getting off: Ueno's Shinkansen platforms are on B4. Take the escalator or elevator up to the B3 concourse, then keep going up to the ground floor (1F). Long escalators — use the elevator for the member with the cane and the big bags.
  2. At the Shinkansen exit gate on 1F, feed the fare ticket and the Kagayaki ticket together. The gate keeps both — nothing comes back.
  3. Head for the Main Entrance (正面玄関口) on the Central Gate side. A recent hotel guest's directions: from the main exit, cross the street, turn left, then right. They warn that Google's suggested "Exit 3" has a lot of stairs — avoid it with luggage.
  4. It's about 3–7 minutes on foot. If anyone's tired, the taxi rank is right outside — one short ride.
