2 · 9:15 AM · don't miss this one

JR Nikko Line to Utsunomiya 日光線

Local train · about 45 min · no reserved seats
Route
  1. At JR Nikko Station: the taxi stops at the front door of the small white station building. The ticket gates are straight ahead inside; restrooms and a waiting room are in the same hall.
  2. Feed the fare ticket only into the gate and take it back out on the far side. If you would rather, show it at the staffed window beside the gates.
  3. The platform is right behind the gates on the same level — no stairs. Every train here goes to Utsunomiya; check the board for 宇都宮 / Utsunomiya 9:15.
  4. The train is usually waiting with its doors closed: press the button beside the door to open it. Sit anywhere (all seats are unreserved) and keep the suitcases by the doors or in front of your knees.
  5. Stay on to the last stop, Utsunomiya (about 45 min, 6 stops). Everyone gets off there.
3 · 10:35 AM

Yamabiko 130 to Omiya 宇都宮駅 → 大宮駅

Utsunomiya Track 4 · car 6 · 24 min
Route
  1. Getting off the Nikko Line: you arrive at the ground-level platforms (normally Track 5). Take the escalator, stairs or elevator up one floor to the concourse that crosses above the tracks.
  2. Do not go out through the main exit gates. Inside the concourse, follow the green signs 新幹線 / Shinkansen to the Shinkansen transfer gates (新幹線のりかえ口).
  3. Feed the fare ticket and the Yamabiko ticket together (stacked, either order) into the transfer gate and collect both on the far side.
  4. You are now in the Shinkansen area: restrooms, a waiting room and a kiosk are here. You have about 30 minutes, so this is the comfortable place to wait.
  5. Around 10:20, go up one more floor to Track 4 (東京方面 / for Tokyo, Omiya) — escalator or elevator. Track 1 on the other side goes north, the wrong way.
  6. On the platform, find the floor and overhead markers for car 6 (6号車) and line up there. Check the board shows やまびこ 130 / Yamabiko 130 · 10:35.
  7. On board: your seats are on the Yamabiko ticket. Big bags go behind the last row of seats or on the overhead rack. The ride is 24 minutes and Omiya is the first stop — start gathering bags when it is announced.
4 · 10:59–11:13 AM · 14-min change

Change at Omiya for Kagayaki 529 大宮駅

Arrive tracks 13–15 → leave from Track 18 · car 4
Route
  1. Getting off: you arrive on the upper level at one of Tracks 13–15. Kagayaki 529 leaves from Track 18, which is a different platform, so you have to go down and back up.
  2. Take the nearest escalator or elevator down one floor to the Shinkansen concourse. Do not go through any ticket gates — stay inside the Shinkansen area (no tickets needed for this change).
  3. In the concourse, look up for the signs 17・18 (北陸新幹線 / Hokuriku Shinkansen, for Nagano, Kanazawa) and take that escalator or elevator back up.
  4. On the platform, Track 18 is the side signed for Nagano / Kanazawa. Walk to the car 4 (4号車) marker. Cars 1–7 are toward the south (Tokyo) end, the same end you got off at.
  5. Check the board shows かがやき 529 / Kagayaki 529 · 11:13, then, if there is time, buy a bento or drinks from the platform kiosk. Allow 8–10 minutes for the walk; you have 14.
  6. On board: every seat on a Kagayaki is reserved, so sit only in the seats on your ticket. Nagano is the first stop, 58 minutes away.
  7. If you miss it: do not board another train. Go down to the concourse and ask at the staffed window by the gates; they will move you to the next Hokuriku train.
5 · 12:11 PM

Arrive Nagano, drop bags at the hotel ホテルメトロポリタン長野

Hotel Metropolitan Nagano · connected to the station (Zenkoji Exit side)
Directions
  1. Getting off: the Shinkansen platforms are at ground level. Take the escalator or elevator up to the concourse on the 2nd floor.
  2. At the Shinkansen exit gates, feed in all three tickets together. The gate keeps them — nothing comes back.
  3. Turn toward the Zenkoji Exit (善光寺口), the west side of the station, and follow the signs for Hotel Metropolitan Nagano / ホテルメトロポリタン長野. The hotel building is joined to the station on this side, a 1–2 minute walk; ask station staff if you do not see the sign.
  4. At the front desk, say you have two reservations (tonight, then Nov 15–18) and ask to keep the same room for all four nights.
  5. Leave the luggage with the desk and ask what time the room will be ready (usually mid-afternoon). Lunch is back inside the station building, on MIDORI 3F.
6 · ~12:30–1:30 PM

Lunch: Shinshu soba 信州そば

MIDORI building 3F, inside the station
Find nearby
  • Nagano is soba country. On MIDORI 3F: Kusabue (big portions, walnut dipping sauce; lunch until 3 PM) and Miyota next door (open all afternoon, English menu).
  • Not in a noodle mood: Meijitei on the same floor does sauce katsudon.
  • Two minutes outside the station, Ten serves 100% buckwheat soba with vegetarian and gluten-free options.
  • Saturday lunch lines are normal — put your name on the list and wait on the benches.

Errands around the station 長野駅

All flat and within a 5-min walk
Directions
  • Daiso: 6th floor of the Nagano Tokyu department store, across from the Zenkoji Exit (10 AM–8 PM). Small but has the basics.
  • JR ticket office (みどりの窓口) in the station: buy the Nov 18 Shinano seats here if you don't have them yet.
  • MIDORI lower floors: local snacks, apples, oyaki dumplings, and a supermarket for tomorrow's breakfast.
  • No Uniqlo near the station — the closest is about 3 km away by taxi.
