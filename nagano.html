1 · 8:20–8:30 AM

Check out, taxi to JR Nikko JR日光駅

Two taxis for six with bags · ~7 min · about ¥1,000–1,500 each
Directions
  • Pay and check out at the 2F front desk by about 8:20; the taxi was booked for 8:30.
  • Tell the driver "JR Nikko eki" — Tobu Nikko is a different station on the same street.
  • Have the three Nov 14 tickets on top: fare ticket, Yamabiko, Kagayaki. Cash for the taxi.
2 · 9:15 AM · don't miss this one

JR Nikko Line to Utsunomiya 日光線

Local train · about 45 min · no reserved seats
  • At the gate, feed in the fare ticket only and take it back.
  • The train waits at the platform before leaving — board early and keep the bags by the doors.
  • Restroom at the station before boarding; the ride has no food service.
3 · 10:35 AM

Yamabiko 130 to Omiya 宇都宮駅 → 大宮駅

Utsunomiya Track 4 · car 6 · 24 min
  • About 35 minutes to change. Follow the Shinkansen signs to the transfer gate (新幹線のりかえ口) and feed in the fare ticket + Yamabiko ticket together; take both back.
  • Elevators go up to the platform. Stand at the car 6 floor marking.
  • Time for a drink or a snack from the concourse shops before going up.
4 · 10:59–11:13 AM · 14-min change

Change at Omiya for Kagayaki 529 大宮駅

Arrive tracks 13–15 → leave from Track 18 · car 4
  • 14 minutes, and you stay inside the Shinkansen area — no gate. Go down to the concourse, then up the escalator or elevator marked 17–18.
  • Go straight to Track 18 first, then shop: there are kiosks on the platform level for a bento.
  • Platforms can change — check the departure board for かがやき 529 / Kagayaki 529.
5 · 12:11 PM

Arrive Nagano, drop bags at the hotel ホテルメトロポリタン長野

Hotel Metropolitan Nagano · connected to the station (Zenkoji Exit side)
Directions
  • At the exit gate, feed in all three tickets — the gate keeps them.
  • Leave the luggage at the front desk; rooms are usually ready from mid-afternoon (ask what time).
  • There are two reservations (Nov 14, then Nov 15–18) — ask to keep the same room for all four nights.
6 · ~12:30–1:30 PM

Lunch: Shinshu soba 信州そば

MIDORI building 3F, inside the station
Find nearby
  • Nagano is soba country. On MIDORI 3F: Kusabue (big portions, walnut dipping sauce; lunch until 3 PM) and Miyota next door (open all afternoon, English menu).
  • Not in a noodle mood: Meijitei on the same floor does sauce katsudon.
  • Two minutes outside the station, Ten serves 100% buckwheat soba with vegetarian and gluten-free options.
  • Saturday lunch lines are normal — put your name on the list and wait on the benches.
7 · ~1:45–3:00 PM

Errands around the station 長野駅

All flat and within a 5-min walk
Directions
  • Daiso: 6th floor of the Nagano Tokyu department store, across from the Zenkoji Exit (10 AM–8 PM). Small but has the basics.
  • JR ticket office (みどりの窓口) in the station: buy the Nov 18 Shinano seats here if you don't have them yet.
  • MIDORI lower floors: local snacks, apples, oyaki dumplings, and a supermarket for tomorrow's breakfast.
  • No Uniqlo near the station — the closest is about 3 km away by taxi.
8 · ~3:00 PM

Check in and rest チェックイン

Superior Twin · coin laundry in the hotel
Directions
  • Good afternoon for laundry — this is the first coin laundry since Tokyo.
  • Charge the pocket WiFi and phones.
  • Sunset is about 4:40 PM. Zenkoji is tomorrow, so there is nothing you need to rush out for.
9 · ~6:00 PM

Dinner near the station 夕食

MIDORI 3F restaurants stay open until about 9:30–10 PM
Find nearby
  • Easy option: back to MIDORI 3F. Local things to try: oyaki, Shinshu salmon, apple desserts, sake.
  • Evening will be about 5–8°C — a sweater and jacket for any walk outside.

Tonight — set up tomorrow

  • Breakfast: tonight's booking doesn't include it (breakfast comes with the Nov 15–18 booking). Confirm at the desk, and pick up something from the station supermarket or plan a café.
  • Tomorrow (Nov 15): Zenkoji Temple and Nakamise Street. Rain is possible, so put the folding umbrella in the day bag.
  • Takayama room: free changes to the Nov 18–22 booking end tomorrow night — decide on a second room before then.
  • If a train is missed: go to the staffed ticket window before boarding anything else; reserved seats can be moved to a later train once, free.
